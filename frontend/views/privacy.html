Overview

LearnPremium ("we", "us", or "our") provides online courses, lessons, and an in-browser Python playground. This Privacy Policy applies to our website, learning dashboard, and related services.

By creating an account or using the platform, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never shown back to us.
  • Profile information: any optional details you add to your account.
  • Learning activity: courses you enroll in, lessons you open, and lessons you mark as complete.
  • Support messages: information you send when you contact us for help.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, buttons used, and general interactions with lessons and the code playground.
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, approximate region, and error logs used to keep the service stable.
  • Payment records: the plan purchased, amount, status, and transaction reference. Card and mobile money details are handled by our payment provider, not stored on our servers.

Code you run in the playground

Python code is executed in your browser using Pyodide (WebAssembly). Your code and its output are not sent to our servers by the playground. Be careful not to paste sensitive information into code examples or shared screenshots.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • Provide courses, lessons, progress tracking, and certificates or completion records where available.
  • Process payments, confirm subscriptions, and prevent fraudulent transactions.
  • Respond to support requests and send important service notices.
  • Improve lesson content, fix bugs, and understand which features are useful.
  • Protect the platform, enforce our Terms of Service, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your email address to send marketing messages unless you have opted in, and you can opt out at any time.

Sharing and disclosure

We only share information with service providers that help us run LearnPremium, or when required by law. These may include:

  • Payment processors: to complete checkout and manage subscriptions securely.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: to serve the website, API, and databases.
  • Content delivery networks: to load fonts, icons, and the Python runtime used by the playground.
  • Legal authorities: when we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with a valid legal request or to protect rights and safety.

Service providers are expected to protect your information and to use it only for the services they provide to us.

Storage and security

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and restricted access to production systems. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your login details private.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep the site working smoothly:

  • Authentication token: stored in local storage so you stay signed in between pages.
  • Theme preference: stored so the site remembers light or dark mode.
  • Essential cookies: used by the hosting and payment experience to keep sessions secure.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing them may sign you out or reset your preferences.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children's privacy

LearnPremium is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information.

Third-party services

The platform may link to third-party websites or use third-party tools, including payment checkout, analytics, icon libraries, and the Pyodide runtime for Python. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may notify you by email or through the platform. Continued use of the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at support@learnpremium.com.

Your data, your choices

You can update your profile, clear your browser storage, or request account deletion at any time. See our Terms of Service for the rules that apply when using LearnPremium.
